# 🛒 Matjari (متجري) — Full POS System for Retail Shops

A full point-of-sale system designed for small to medium shops, with every detail a manager needs. Track products and inventory, record sales and orders, manage customer debts and employees, and review financial reports — all from one dashboard.

Live: [matjari-dashboard.vercel.app](https://matjari-dashboard.vercel.app)

## ✨ Highlights

- **Dashboard** — daily revenue, sales count, net profit, pending orders, outstanding debts, product/employee counts
- **Products** — names, barcodes, categories, cost prices, quantities, low-stock alerts
- **Sales & POS** — barcode scanning, cart, sales history, date-range filtering
- **Orders** — customer details, delivery fees, notes, status flow (pending → confirmed → preparing → ready → delivered)
- **Customer debts** — amounts, due dates, payments, notes
- **Stock movements** — additions, damage/loss tracking, movement history
- **Employees & stores** — multi-store support, role-based access, subscription plans
- **Financial reports** — revenue, profit, loss, and salary summaries

## 👥 Roles

- **Store owner** — full management access: products, inventory, sales, orders, debts, employees, stores, reports, subscriptions
- **Employee** — day-to-day operations: sales, orders, stock movements (scoped by role and plan)
